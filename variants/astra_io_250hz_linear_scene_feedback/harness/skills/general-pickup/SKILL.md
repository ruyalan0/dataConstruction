name: general-pickup
description: Identify the instructed object and lift it at least 10 cm while retaining it.
---

Read the current instruction and identify its target from the RGB views before touching it. Record distinguishing appearance, location and unresolved identity in memory. Resolve ambiguity with another permitted view; every scene requires its own identification.

Choose an arm with visible clearance for the fingers, wrist and target. Use joint-goal waypoints for alignment, descent and closure, then inspect a short test lift. Keep the other arm clear, or use its wrist camera from a clear viewpoint when helpful. Reassess approach or grasp geometry if the target stays on its support or separates from the fingers.

Raise the target object at least 10 cm above its starting height while maintaining retention. Wrist rise alone cannot establish object rise. Compare the target against fingers and background in the paired views, and allow a clearance-compatible margin for visual uncertainty. Keep the target held above the required height while normal observations establish native termination.

This task requires neither placement nor home return. If retention is lost, relocalize the same instructed target in its current supported pose and revise the grasp. Occlusion leaves the outcome uncertain; seek a useful view instead of substituting another object or declaring success.
