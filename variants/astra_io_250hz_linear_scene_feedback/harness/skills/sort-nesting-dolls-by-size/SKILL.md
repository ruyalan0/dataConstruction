Choose a clear horizontal row across the table with room for all five bases, gaps between neighboring bodies, and finger access during release. Use current geometry to place the smallest at the left end and largest at the right end. Estimate the overall span before moving the first doll. Keep a shared front-to-back alignment for their base centers; a diagonal row in world space is not the requested horizontal row even if perspective makes it look straight. Preserve dolls already correctly placed when the remaining moves remain clear.

Plan destinations and temporary support so an unsorted doll cannot block a later slot. For each move compare source and destination sides and use cross-side-handoff when different arms are needed. Keep any staged doll upright, maintain its identity through regrasp and keep the completed part of the row clear. Record settled slots and the next intended doll after each placement.

Grasp an opposing region of the body that fits between the pads. Choose contact height from the actual doll shape: a narrow curved head can slip and a very low grasp can catch the table. If it slips or tilts, return it to visible support and revise contact before another transfer.

Center the base over its planned slot, lower vertically in short observed segments and release once the base is supported. Withdraw without brushing neighboring dolls, then verify it stands independently with visible gaps. A leaning or touching placement needs correction before filling the adjacent slot. When a configuration obstructs approach or withdrawal, preserve support before empty-hand posture adjustment; healthy compact standby alone is acceptable.

With fingers clear, recheck all five physical sizes in left-to-right order, upright posture, gaps and the straight alignment of base centers. Correct a misplaced doll using a clear temporary support area rather than pushing a row of rounded bodies. Open both grippers, return both arms to their recorded original poses and use normal verification until native termination.
