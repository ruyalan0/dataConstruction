Inspect all three mugs, their handle openings, the rack hooks and clear approach directions from current RGB. Assign a free hook to each mug using the handle opening and hook direction, not just mug-center proximity. Record completed hanging mugs and occupied hooks. Choose the arm that can both pick and approach the chosen hook; use cross-side-handoff on visible table support when the endpoints require different arms.

Grasp the mug body at opposing surfaces while leaving its handle opening accessible. Confirm retention with a short lift. Keep the mug and wrist clear of neighboring mugs and the rack. Reorient in visible free space in short observed increments so the hook can enter the handle opening; use head and active-wrist views to resolve the hook tip relative to both sides of the handle.

Before entering the rack, identify the handle opening, its plane, the free hook tip and the unobstructed direction from that tip into the opening. Align the hook shaft through the opening, with the mug body and both fingers outside that path. A visible handle silhouette alone is insufficient when its hole is edge-on or hidden behind a finger. Reorient in free space until the opening and tip can be compared in at least one current view; use the other view to check depth. Aim the handle hole rather than the mug center or wrist position.

After two approaches disturb finger opening or reseat the mug without visible hook passage, record the failed approach and withdraw clear. Choose a materially different handle-plane orientation, a free hook with an accessible insertion direction, or a supported regrasp on the side of the body opposite the handle. Small lateral offsets with the same hidden opening do not resolve this failure. For a regrasp, first place the mug stably on visible table support, verify release, and acquire the new body grasp with a short retention lift. Resume insertion only once the hook entry and finger clearance are observable.

Approach the hook with the handle opening slightly above its supporting section. Advance only a short segment at a time until the hook visibly passes through the opening, then lower gently until the mug weight is supported. If the handle contacts the tip or shaft without engagement, retreat along the approach direction and correct alignment before trying again. A cup next to the rack or resting against it is not confirmed hanging.

Open only after supported engagement appears established, then withdraw along a path that clears both handle and hook. Verify that the mug remains hanging as the fingers separate and move away. Preserve completed mugs while addressing the remaining hooks. Finish with all three hanging, both hands open and both arms returned to their supplied original poses. Read the live remaining native-step budget before each new transfer and reserve withdrawal and reset time.
