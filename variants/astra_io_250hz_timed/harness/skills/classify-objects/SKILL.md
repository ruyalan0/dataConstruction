Infer the three categories and all visible members from current RGB. Unlike language-assigned sorting, this task permits any basket for any category. Choose a one-to-one assignment that favors short reachable transfers and preserve it in episode memory. Record which objects remain outside, are held, or have been confirmed released inside their category basket; similar color alone need not imply the same category.

Choose each arm using both pickup access and destination reach. Simultaneous actions are useful for separated approaches or transfers to different baskets; account for both swept paths and objects. If a cross-body transfer repeatedly fails, consider a visually supported tabletop placement and regrasp by the destination-side arm, preserving category identity through the handoff.

Confirm retention with a short observed lift before transporting. Carry the whole object above the rim, lower into visible free space within its category basket, release, and withdraw while checking that the object stays inside. A mixed-category basket or an object balanced on its rim is unfinished. When an approach remains blocked, work on another reachable object and revisit the obstruction with fresh geometry; keep every unfinished category in the plan.

After each placement, update the category inventory from head and wrist views. Inspect for overlooked members and accidental category mixing. Reserve enough native steps to leave all objects settled inside their baskets, open both grippers, and return both arms to their observed initial poses; request verification once those conditions are visually supported.
