name: imitate-sorting-sequence
description: Observe the opposite robot's five-object placement sequence, remember visual identities and replay that order with corresponding objects in RoboDojo imitate_sorting_sequence.
---

Begin in observation mode with both policy arms held at their supplied initial poses. Identify the opposite demonstrator, its source objects and basket, and the corresponding policy-side objects and policy basket from current RGB. Record distinctive appearance cues for each object category and its policy-side location. The two baskets have different roles; demonstration objects stay in the demonstration basket throughout.

Keep both policy arms stationary throughout the demonstration, including orientation and gripper commands. Use control_arms with both arms null and unchanged gripper commands to advance the controller observation window and obtain fresh views. API waiting alone does not advance the simulator. Compare successive frames for the demonstrator's pickup, carried object and release. Record each completed placement as an ordered visual identity, supporting step/image reference and confidence. A stationary frame or the final unordered basket contents do not establish placement order.

Track the current demonstrated object separately from the confirmed sequence. Append it once when release into the demonstration basket is observed and the demonstrator withdraws; avoid counting the same carried object again in the next frame. Match by category, shape and distinctive appearance across the two sides rather than by initial image position alone. Keep uncertain identities explicit and use subsequent unobstructed views to resolve them. Preserve the complete observation ledger across controller continuation; do not reconstruct missing order from a seed, trajectory file or hidden state.

Remain at home until all five placements are observed and the demonstrator visibly returns clear and stays idle across fresh views. If the demonstration visibly drops an object outside its basket or the sequence remains incomplete or ambiguous, record that observation and keep the uncertainty unresolved rather than inventing an order or moving the demonstrator's objects. Native validity is reported by the harness, not inferred as policy permission to repair the demonstration.

Once the full sequence is confirmed and the demonstrator is finished, select only the policy object matching the next entry. Use visual-contact-control for its actual shape, observed grasp closure and a retention lift, then transport-retention to carry it. Plan cross-side-handoff when source and policy-basket reach require different arms, keeping staging away from both baskets and all later sequence objects. Leave later objects outside the policy basket until their turns.

At demonstration end, divide the remaining simulation time between the pending transfers and final reset. Recompute that allowance after each placement. Establish source identities, arm choice and free basket landing regions during stationary observation so they do not require repeated planning holds later. Use clear-space joint-goal waypoints for already observed clear paths and short segments for uncertain contact. Once retention or supported release is established, proceed to the next phase instead of repeating unchanged settling holds. The observation sequence remains mandatory: save steps in transfer execution, not by guessing the unseen end of the demonstration.

Place each selected object into visible free interior space without catching the basket rim or displacing already placed items. Lower with observed clearance, open and withdraw; confirm the object remains inside before incrementing the completed-policy prefix. Keep both grippers open at this verification point. A grasp or transit is not a completed sequence entry. Preserve the prefix and pending object across interruptions, checking fresh RGB before retrying any release.

After five confirmed placements in the remembered order, verify the policy basket contains all corresponding objects and the demonstration basket remains undisturbed. Open both grippers, return both arms to their original poses and check native completion. If it remains absent after settling, follow the common observation-grounding procedure. Reserve native steps for the initial demonstration, five transfers and final reset.
