name: arrange-largest-number
description: Read the visible digits and place them in descending order on the pads in RoboDojo arrange_largest_number, preserving valid orientation and returning both arms to origin.
---

Before moving pieces, read every visible digit and locate every destination pad in the task's left-to-right frame. Count the actual digits and pads: layouts can contain four or five digits. Resolve ambiguous symbols, including rotated 6 and 9, with another permitted RGB view before committing to the assignment. Sort the single digits in descending numeric order to obtain the largest possible number, and map each digit to its corresponding pad from left to right. Keep this mapping, the intended number and observed completed placements in episode memory. Reconstruct it from each new scene.

The public task requires valid digit orientation as well as correct pad assignment, and notes a permitted rotational tolerance relative to the initial pose. Preserve each piece's observed initial orientation during grasp and transport; plan the gripper closing direction before contact. Place the digit flat and upright in the intended reading frame, correcting any visibly acquired rotation with an observed reorientation. Treat 0 and 8 as digits with a defined reading alignment too. A correctly ordered but sideways or flipped piece is not a completed placement. Keep a per-digit orientation correction pending when staging or contact visibly rotates it. “No rotation since the receiving grasp” does not resolve that earlier change. Before release, compare the digit’s top/bottom and main stroke direction against the original reading frame; after open-hand withdrawal, verify the unoccluded shape and correct a sideways digit before marking its pad complete.

Choose arms from current reach, clearance and grasp posture. Apply upright-grasp-approach before fine descent, visual-contact-control for opposing contact and a short test lift, and transport-retention while carrying. Prepare or transport two digits simultaneously when their arm paths and destination approaches are separated. If a pad is occupied by the wrong digit, first relocate that piece to visible reachable support so the intended placement has clear space. Preserve correctly placed pieces while handling their neighbors.

Center each digit on its assigned pad, lower in short observed segments, release onto support and retreat through visible clearance. Verify that the intended digit remains centered and correctly oriented as the fingers leave. Update the placement mapping from the resulting RGB rather than the commanded motion.

Completion requires every digit on its assigned pad in descending order with valid orientation, both grippers open and both arms returned to their supplied original poses. Re-read the completed number across the entire pad row, resolve any occluded placement with a permitted view, and reserve actions for return and normal native-termination verification.
