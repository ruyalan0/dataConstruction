---
name: plug-in-charger
description: Align the held plug and socket holes using the other wrist view before controlled insertion.
---

Inspect the plug, pin arrangement, socket holes and cable slack. Choose a plug-body grasp that leaves the pins exposed and does not pull against the cable. Test-lift and orient in clear space so the pins approach along the socket-hole axes.

Before insertion, move the EMPTY arm to a noncontact observation pose that resolves both pin tips and socket holes. The holding wrist often occludes exactly the lateral or angular error that matters. Use the empty wrist plus head view to check the complete pin pattern, plug roll, parallelism and approach depth. Keep the observing hand, camera and its arm outside the plug/cable sweep. Move the observer again if the insertion path becomes occluded.

Only after alignment is supported by these views, advance a short segment along the insertion axis. If one pin contacts the face, the plug tilts or progress stalls, withdraw clear enough to remove contact, correct alignment, and reapproach. Do not use high force or repeated blind pushes to search for holes. Keep the other wrist watching during final seating. Release only after the plug is visibly engaged and supported by the socket; withdraw without pulling the cable, inspect that engagement persists, then return both arms home.

Evidence: task-specific development revision from the operator's 2026-09-21 review and prior failure observations. This is a strategy candidate, not a demonstrated success; resolve concrete poses from the current episode only.
